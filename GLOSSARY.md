# Instrument geometry

Terms for interpreting instrument descriptions when estimating modeled access.

## Language

**Geometry candidate**:
A numeric width or angle extracted from a source description. Successful extraction alone establishes no compatibility with the coverage model.

**Supported geometry**:
Instrument geometry with positive source evidence of a continuous swath centered beneath the satellite, without gaps or separate pointing needed to reach the stated width. Relevant descriptions across the instrument record agree with that representation for access at a ground point.

**Approximate geometry**:
Instrument geometry represented by a model with a known mismatch. The mismatch and its effect on interpreting modeled access are stated explicitly.
